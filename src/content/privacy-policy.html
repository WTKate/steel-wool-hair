Privacy Policy

How Steel Wool Hair in Belrose collects, uses, stores and handles personal information when you contact us or use our website.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. About this policy

Steel Wool Hair (we, us and our) operates a hairdressing salon at 6 Everton Road, Belrose NSW 2085, Australia. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website, enquiries and salon services. If you have a question about privacy, contact us at steelwoolhair@hotmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

When you use our contact form, email us, telephone us or visit the salon, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, the details of your enquiry, appointment information and information you choose to share with us about the services you request. We may also receive technical information generated when you visit our website, such as device, browser and usage information collected through website hosting, security features, cookies or similar technologies.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, arrange and manage appointments, provide salon services, communicate about your visit, maintain records, protect the security and operation of our website and comply with applicable legal obligations. We may send promotional communications where permitted by law; you can ask us to stop at any time.

4. Website forms and third-party services

Our website uses Wix technology, including a Wix contact form. Information submitted through that form may be processed and stored using Wix services so we can respond. Appointment booking links may take you to Kitomba, and the Online Store link takes you to a Keune website. Those external providers operate their own services and privacy policies. If you make a booking or purchase on another website, please review that provider's terms and privacy information before submitting details. We do not collect payment card details through our website's contact form.

5. Cookies and technical information

Our website and service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security and, where enabled, performance or analytics. You can manage cookies using your browser settings, although disabling some cookies may affect site functionality. Third-party sites linked from our website may use their own cookies.

6. Disclosure and overseas processing

We may share information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, process enquiries, manage bookings, provide services or meet legal requirements. Providers of hosting, forms, booking or other technology services may process or store information outside Australia. We take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances when selecting and working with service providers.

7. Storage, security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against misuse, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably required for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law, and then take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it when appropriate.

8. Access, correction and complaints

You can contact us to request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will consider requests and complaints in accordance with applicable law. If the Australian Privacy Principles apply to our handling of your information and you are dissatisfied with our response, you may be entitled to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy from time to time by publishing a revised version on this page. For privacy questions or requests, email steelwoolhair@hotmail.com or write to Steel Wool Hair, 6 Everton Road, Belrose NSW 2085, Australia.
