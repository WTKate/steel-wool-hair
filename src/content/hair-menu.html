Meet Sylvia

Meet Sylvia.

Silv has been in the industry for over 20 years with experience in all types of hairdressing, including working in premier salons in London for two years before returning to Australia to get married and start her family.

In today’s hurried world, it’s lovely to find a hairdresser such as Silv who will spend time with you, making sure you understand the best options for your hair, cut, style and products.

She has experience working with children and neurodiverse clients, so feel free to talk to Silv about your situation and preferences.
